Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. OpenFlow is MIT-licensed open source, and the speech models it downloads are open-weight. There is no account, subscription or usage limit. The optional Groq key is also free.

Does my audio leave my computer?

Not by default. Everything runs locally, and history is stored only on your machine. If you add a Groq key and choose cloud mode, audio is sent to Groq for transcription.

What hardware do I need?

Any 64-bit Windows PC works. Setup picks smaller models for computers without an NVIDIA GPU, so it stays usable, just slower. Plan for about 5 GB of disk space with an NVIDIA GPU (models plus CUDA engines) and about 1.5 GB without one.

How accurate is it?

In our own tests on a minute of natural speech, it got about 99% of words right, including names, numbers, dates and Indian currency like "2.5 lakhs". Add names and jargon to your personal dictionary to help it further.

How do I uninstall it?

Run openflow autostart off and openflow stop, then delete the OpenFlow folder in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs and the Start menu shortcut.
